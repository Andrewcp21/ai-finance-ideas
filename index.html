AI Finance Tools Ideas

Finance & FP&A

6 ideas

Margin Leakage Finder

Finds unapproved discounts, prices below list and loss-making sales in monthly sales data, and estimates the profit that can be recovered.

The problem: Unapproved discounts and loss-making sales hide in sales data until the year-end results.

Who uses it
FP&A analyst, sales controller
When
Monthly, after sales close
Input
Sales export, price list, standard costs
Output
Flagged deals and the profit you can recover

How it works

  1. 1Upload the month's sales export, price list and standard costs.
  2. 2Review the flagged invoice lines and the customers leaking the most margin.
  3. 3Send the draft actions to the sales team.

Who decides: The finance manager approves actions before they go to sales.

More Finance & FP&A ideas

Month-End Commentary Drafter

Drafts variance commentary for every line over the materiality threshold and lists questions for line owners where the cause is unknown.

The problem: Writing variance commentary every close takes hours, and causes are chased by email.

Who uses it
FP&A analyst
When
Monthly, at close
Input
Actuals vs budget export, materiality threshold, business unit notes
Output
Draft commentary per flagged line and questions for line owners

How it works

  1. 1Upload actuals vs budget.
  2. 2Review the lines over the threshold and the draft comments.
  3. 3Edit inline and copy the final commentary.

Who decides: The analyst confirms every cause. Unknown causes stay marked, not guessed.

Scenario Forecaster

Lets you move key drivers such as growth, receivable days and FX, and shows the impact on profit, cash and covenants for each scenario.

The problem: Management asks what-if questions that need the model rerun quickly.

Who uses it
FP&A manager, CFO
When
When management asks a what-if question
Input
Key figures from the three-statement model, scenario assumptions
Output
Profit, cash and covenant impact per scenario, with a summary table

How it works

  1. 1Enter the base figures.
  2. 2Move the driver sliders for each scenario.
  3. 3Compare scenarios and read the summary of what moved.

Who decides: FP&A reconciles the results to the full model before sharing.

Cash vs Profit Explainer

Explains in plain language why profit and operating cash moved differently, with a profit-to-cash bridge.

The problem: Business heads ask why profit went up but cash went down.

Who uses it
FP&A analyst, business heads
When
Monthly or quarterly reporting
Input
Key P&L and balance sheet lines for two periods
Output
Profit-to-cash bridge chart and the top three drivers in plain language

How it works

  1. 1Enter figures for two periods.
  2. 2See the bridge from net profit to operating cash.
  3. 3Share the explanation with business heads.

Who decides: Finance checks that every number ties back to the statements.

Board / CFO Memo Writer

Turns analysis into a one-page memo with the decision needed, options, numbers, risks and next steps.

The problem: Good analysis gets lost in long decks.

Who uses it
Finance manager, FP&A
When
Before a board or CFO decision
Input
Decision needed, options, key numbers, audience
Output
One-page memo in a fixed template

How it works

  1. 1Fill in the decision, options and key numbers.
  2. 2Review the draft memo.
  3. 3Edit, then copy or download it.

Who decides: The owner checks every figure and the tone before sending.

CFO Sparring Partner

Plays a sceptical CFO who challenges a proposal before the real meeting.

The problem: Proposals get rejected on questions nobody prepared for.

Who uses it
Anyone presenting to the CFO
When
Before a capital request or proposal review
Input
Draft proposal or capital plan
Output
Tough questions, weak assumptions and missing data, ranked by risk

How it works

  1. 1Paste the proposal.
  2. 2Answer the questions one at a time.
  3. 3Fix the weak points listed at the end.

Who decides: You decide which challenges are valid.

Corporate Finance

4 ideas

13-Week Cash Flow Forecaster

Builds a weekly cash forecast for the next 13 weeks, shows the weeks that fall below minimum cash, and suggests actions to close the gap.

The problem: Cash forecasts are built by hand, so shortfalls are seen too late to act.

Who uses it
Treasury or finance manager, CFO
When
Weekly
Input
Bank balances, receivables aging, payables schedule, payroll and tax dates
Output
13-week cash forecast, shortfall weeks and actions to close the gap

How it works

  1. 1Upload bank balances and the receivables and payables lists.
  2. 2See which weeks fall below minimum cash.
  3. 3Choose actions to close the gap: collections, payment timing or a credit line.

Who decides: The CFO chooses which actions to take.

More Corporate Finance ideas

Funding Decision Analyst

Compares a bank loan, shareholder loan and equity for a project, including after-tax cost and the 4 : 1 debt-to-equity check.

The problem: Choosing between a bank loan, shareholder loan and equity is done with incomplete tax and legal input.

Who uses it
Treasury, CFO
When
Before choosing how to fund a project
Input
Project cash flows, funding terms, current debt and equity
Output
NPV, IRR, payback and after-tax cost per option, with tax and approval flags

How it works

  1. 1Enter the project cash flows and each funding option.
  2. 2Compare the options side by side.
  3. 3Read the flags for transfer pricing and approvals.

Who decides: The tax team confirms tax treatment and treasury confirms rates.

Covenant Monitor

Tracks each loan covenant against its limit every month and warns before a breach.

The problem: Covenant breaches are spotted late.

Who uses it
Treasury, finance manager
When
Monthly, after the financials close
Input
Covenant formulas and limits (set once), monthly financials
Output
Headroom and red, amber or green status per covenant, with history

How it works

  1. 1Set up each covenant once from the loan agreement.
  2. 2Enter the month's figures.
  3. 3Act early on any amber or red covenant.

Who decides: Treasury checks ratio definitions against the agreements.

Group Cash Position Summary

Shows cash across all group entities, flags entities short of cash, and suggests intercompany funding moves.

The problem: Cash sits in some entities while others borrow.

Who uses it
Group treasury
When
Weekly
Input
Bank balances, committed payments and forecasts per entity
Output
Group cash view, entities short of cash, possible funding moves

How it works

  1. 1Paste each entity's cash and forecast.
  2. 2See the group total and the entities short of cash.
  3. 3Review the suggested funding moves.

Who decides: Treasury approves any funding move.

Tax

7 ideas

SP2DK Response Prep

Reconciles each item DJP flags in an SP2DK letter, lists the evidence needed, and drafts a reply for the tax manager to review.

The problem: DJP clarification letters (SP2DK) need fast reconciliation and a clear reply.

Who uses it
Tax manager, tax staff
When
When an SP2DK letter arrives
Input
SP2DK letter (PDF), general ledger, e-Faktur export, corporate tax return
Output
Reconciliation per flagged item, evidence checklist, draft reply letter

How it works

  1. 1Upload the letter and the records it refers to.
  2. 2Check the difference and explanation for each flagged item.
  3. 3Collect missing evidence and edit the draft reply.

Who decides: The tax manager or consultant approves the reply before anything is sent to DJP.

More Tax ideas

Tax Regulation Assistant

Answers tax questions only from a loaded set of regulations and shows the article it used.

The problem: Rules change often and staff answer from memory.

Who uses it
Tax staff, finance team
When
Whenever a tax question comes up
Input
A question, plus the regulation texts loaded once
Output
Answer citing the regulation and article, or “not covered”

How it works

  1. 1Load the regulations your team uses.
  2. 2Ask a question in plain language.
  3. 3Check the cited article.

Who decides: Tax staff verify each citation.

Fiscal Correction Finder

Scans the expense ledger for items that often need fiscal correction in the annual corporate tax return.

The problem: Fiscal corrections for the annual corporate tax return are found late.

Who uses it
Tax staff, accountants
When
Before preparing the annual tax return
Input
Trial balance and detailed expense ledger
Output
Likely positive and negative corrections, with the reason and evidence needed

How it works

  1. 1Upload the expense ledger.
  2. 2Review the flagged accounts and reasons.
  3. 3Collect evidence or book the correction.

Who decides: The tax team decides each correction.

The problem: Staff are unsure of the VAT base and invoice code after the 12% change.

Who uses it
Tax staff, billing team
When
Before invoicing an unusual transaction
Input
Transaction description and the parties
Output
VAT base, rate, invoice code and the regulation cited

How it works

  1. 1Answer the guided questions.
  2. 2See the VAT base, rate and code.
  3. 3Read the explanation.

Who decides: Tax staff confirm before invoicing.

Tells accounts payable which PPh article and rate apply to a payment and which documents are needed.

The problem: Accounts payable is unsure which PPh article and rate applies to a payment.

Who uses it
Accounts payable, tax staff
When
Before paying a vendor
Input
Payment type, vendor status and residency, contract summary
Output
PPh article, rate and documents needed

How it works

  2. 2See the article, rate and documents.

Who decides: Tax staff confirm unusual cases.

Transfer Pricing First Draft

Drafts the functional analysis for transfer pricing documentation and checks the documentation thresholds.

The problem: Transfer pricing documentation under PMK 172/2023 takes weeks of drafting.

Who uses it
Tax manager, TP specialist
When
During annual TP documentation
Input
Related-party transactions, group structure, functions and risks per entity
Output
Draft functional analysis, transaction characterisation, threshold check

How it works

  1. 1Fill in the functions, assets and risks checklist.
  2. 2Review the draft analysis.
  3. 3Hand over to the TP specialist for benchmarking.

Who decides: A TP specialist reviews. Benchmarking stays with specialists.

Global Minimum Tax Screener

Checks whether a group is likely in scope of the global minimum tax and lists the data to collect.

The problem: Large groups are unsure whether PMK 136/2024 applies and what data they need.

Who uses it
Group tax
When
Annual planning
Input
Consolidated revenue, profit and taxes paid per jurisdiction
Output
In or out of scope, effective tax rate per jurisdiction, data to collect

How it works

  1. 1Enter group revenue and figures per jurisdiction.
  2. 2See the EUR 750m threshold check and effective tax rates.
  3. 3Collect the listed data.

Who decides: Group tax confirms scope with advisors.

Legal

4 ideas

Pre-Signing Contract Risk Check

Checks a contract's financial, tax and data clauses before signing, rates each one, estimates the cost of bad clauses, and suggests positions to negotiate.

The problem: Contracts are signed before finance sees the financial, tax and data clauses.

Who uses it
Finance manager, legal counsel
When
Before signing a contract
Input
Contract draft (PDF or Word)
Output
Risk rating per clause, cost of bad clauses, positions to negotiate

How it works

  1. 1Upload the contract draft.
  2. 2Review the high and medium risk clauses on payment, penalties, tax and data.
  3. 3Use the suggested positions in the negotiation.

Who decides: Legal counsel and the CFO decide what to accept.

Agreement text and how the transactions are booked
Output
Missing or weak terms and mismatches with the books

Pulls payment terms, penalties, FX clauses and renewal dates from contracts into one table.

Who uses it

When reviewing contracts or upcoming renewals

Accounting

4 ideas

Journal Entry Anomaly Detector

Scans the month's journal entries before close and ranks unusual ones: duplicates, round amounts, weekend postings, entries just under approval limits and cut-off issues.

The problem: Wrong postings, duplicates and cut-off errors are found by auditors or DJP, not by the team.

Who uses it
Chief accountant, controller
When
Monthly, before closing the books
Input
The month's journal entries from the accounting system
Output
Ranked list of unusual entries, with the reason each was flagged

How it works

  1. 1Export the month's journal entries and upload them.
  2. 2Go through the ranked list, starting with the highest-risk entries.
  3. 3Correct or document each entry before close.

Who decides: The controller decides which entries to correct.

Group accountants

Input
Intercompany ledgers from each entity
Output
Matched and unmatched items, differences by cause, draft follow-up emails

How it works

  3. 3Send the follow-up emails.

Who decides: Accountants confirm each adjustment.

Generates consolidation elimination journals from reconciled intercompany balances.

The problem: Consolidation eliminations are prepared by hand each period.

Who uses it
Consolidation team, controller
When
Each consolidation period
Input
Reconciled intercompany balances, ownership percentages
Output
Proposed elimination journals with explanations, as CSV

How it works

  1. 1Enter the balances and ownership.
  2. 2Review each proposed journal.
  3. 3Download it for the ERP.

Who decides: The controller approves journals before posting.

Who uses it

Deals

3 ideas

Deal Check Assistant

Runs an intercompany deal through accounting, tax, transfer pricing and legal checks, including the 4 : 1 debt-to-equity limit, and produces a one-page report for the CFO.

The problem: Intercompany deals reach the CFO before accounting, tax and legal issues are checked.

Who uses it
CFO, tax and legal team
When
Before approving an intercompany deal
Input
Deal terms, entity balance sheets, intercompany agreements
Output
Pass or fail on each check and a one-page report for the CFO

How it works

  1. 1Enter the deal terms and upload the entities' balance sheets.
  2. 2See the result of each check, including the 4 : 1 debt-to-equity limit.
  3. 3Share the one-page report with the CFO.

Who decides: The CFO approves or changes the deal.

More Deals ideas

Due Diligence Red Flag Scanner

Scans a target company's financials for common red flags and drafts questions for the due diligence team.

The problem: Red flags in a target's numbers surface late in due diligence, after time and fees are spent.

Who uses it
Corporate development, deal team
When
Early in an acquisition or investment
Input
Target's financial statements for three years, receivables aging, related-party balances
Output
Red flags with the figures behind them and a list of due diligence questions

How it works

  1. 1Upload the target's financials.
  2. 2Review flags such as customer concentration, old receivables and related-party balances.
  3. 3Send the question list to the target or advisors.

Who decides: The deal lead and advisors judge each flag.

Valuation Cross-Check

Compares a DCF value with the multiples you enter and shows which assumptions drive the value most.

The problem: Valuations rest on a few assumptions that nobody stress-tests before negotiation.

Who uses it
CFO, corporate development
When
Before making or accepting an offer
Input
Target forecast, discount rate assumptions, comparable multiples you enter
Output
Value ranges by method, a football-field chart, the assumptions that move value most

How it works

  1. 1Enter the forecast and assumptions.
  2. 2Add comparable multiples.
  3. 3Compare the ranges and test the key assumptions.

Who decides: The CFO and advisors set the price range.

AI Governance

3 ideas

Data Masking Gate for AI

Masks names, NIK, NPWP and bank accounts in the browser before data is sent to Claude, then restores the real values in the answer on screen only.

The problem: Staff paste salaries, NIK and NPWP numbers into AI tools without masking them.

Who uses it
Anyone in finance using AI
When
Every time data goes into an AI tool
Input
Any table or text with names, NIK, NPWP or bank accounts
Output
A masked version for Claude, with real values restored on your screen only

How it works

  1. 1Paste or upload the data.
  2. 2Check what was masked before it is sent.
  3. 3Read Claude's answer with the real values restored on your screen.

Who decides: You check the masked data before anything is sent.

More AI Governance ideas

AI Output Review Log

Records how each AI output was checked and what was fixed, with a monthly summary of patterns.

The problem: Teams can't show how AI output was checked.

Who uses it
Finance teams using AI, team lead
When
Every time an AI output is used
Input
The AI output used and what the reviewer changed
Output
Log of errors and fixes, counts by error type, monthly summary

How it works

  1. 1Log the output and what was wrong.
  2. 2See error counts over time.
  3. 3Read the monthly summary of patterns.

Who decides: The team lead reviews the log monthly.

Personal Data Inventory

Lists which finance files hold personal data, what kind and why, as a starting point for UU PDP records.

The problem: Finance keeps payroll, vendor and customer files with personal data, but nobody has a list of what is kept where.

Who uses it
Finance manager, data protection officer
When
Quarterly, or before an audit
Input
Column headers and a few masked rows from each finance file
Output
Inventory of personal data by file, with type, purpose and retention questions

How it works

  1. 1Paste each file's headers and a few masked rows.
  2. 2Review the detected data types, such as name, NIK, NPWP and bank account.
  3. 3Fill in purpose and retention, then export the list.

Who decides: The data protection officer confirms the inventory.
