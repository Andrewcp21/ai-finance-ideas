AI Finance Tools Ideas

Finance & FP&A

Margin Leakage Finder

Finds unapproved discounts, prices below list and loss-making sales in monthly sales data, and estimates the profit that can be recovered.

The problem: Unapproved discounts and loss-making sales hide in sales data until the year-end results.

Who uses it
FP&A analyst, sales controller
When
Monthly, after sales close
Input
Sales export, price list, standard costs
Output
Flagged deals and the profit you can recover

How it works

  1. 1Upload the month's sales export, price list and standard costs.
  2. 2Review the flagged invoice lines and the customers leaking the most margin.
  3. 3Send the draft actions to the sales team.

Who decides: The finance manager approves actions before they go to sales.

Corporate Finance

13-Week Cash Flow Forecaster

Builds a weekly cash forecast for the next 13 weeks, shows the weeks that fall below minimum cash, and suggests actions to close the gap.

The problem: Cash forecasts are built by hand, so shortfalls are seen too late to act.

Who uses it
Treasury or finance manager, CFO
When
Weekly
Input
Bank balances, receivables aging, payables schedule, payroll and tax dates
Output
13-week cash forecast, shortfall weeks and actions to close the gap

How it works

  1. 1Upload bank balances and the receivables and payables lists.
  2. 2See which weeks fall below minimum cash.
  3. 3Choose actions to close the gap: collections, payment timing or a credit line.

Who decides: The CFO chooses which actions to take.

Tax

SP2DK Response Prep

Reconciles each item DJP flags in an SP2DK letter, lists the evidence needed, and drafts a reply for the tax manager to review.

The problem: DJP clarification letters (SP2DK) need fast reconciliation and a clear reply.

Who uses it
Tax manager, tax staff
When
When an SP2DK letter arrives
Input
SP2DK letter (PDF), general ledger, e-Faktur export, corporate tax return
Output
Reconciliation per flagged item, evidence checklist, draft reply letter

How it works

  1. 1Upload the letter and the records it refers to.
  2. 2Check the difference and explanation for each flagged item.
  3. 3Collect missing evidence and edit the draft reply.

Who decides: The tax manager or consultant approves the reply before anything is sent to DJP.

Legal

Pre-Signing Contract Risk Check

Checks a contract's financial, tax and data clauses before signing, rates each one, estimates the cost of bad clauses, and suggests positions to negotiate.

The problem: Contracts are signed before finance sees the financial, tax and data clauses.

Who uses it
Finance manager, legal counsel
When
Before signing a contract
Input
Contract draft (PDF or Word)
Output
Risk rating per clause, cost of bad clauses, positions to negotiate

How it works

  1. 1Upload the contract draft.
  2. 2Review the high and medium risk clauses on payment, penalties, tax and data.
  3. 3Use the suggested positions in the negotiation.

Who decides: Legal counsel and the CFO decide what to accept.

Accounting

Journal Entry Anomaly Detector

Scans the month's journal entries before close and ranks unusual ones: duplicates, round amounts, weekend postings, entries just under approval limits and cut-off issues.

The problem: Wrong postings, duplicates and cut-off errors are found by auditors or DJP, not by the team.

Who uses it
Chief accountant, controller
When
Monthly, before closing the books
Input
The month's journal entries from the accounting system
Output
Ranked list of unusual entries, with the reason each was flagged

How it works

  1. 1Export the month's journal entries and upload them.
  2. 2Go through the ranked list, starting with the highest-risk entries.
  3. 3Correct or document each entry before close.

Who decides: The controller decides which entries to correct.

Deals

Deal Check Assistant

Runs an intercompany deal through accounting, tax, transfer pricing and legal checks, including the 4 : 1 debt-to-equity limit, and produces a one-page report for the CFO.

The problem: Intercompany deals reach the CFO before accounting, tax and legal issues are checked.

Who uses it
CFO, tax and legal team
When
Before approving an intercompany deal
Input
Deal terms, entity balance sheets, intercompany agreements
Output
Pass or fail on each check and a one-page report for the CFO

How it works

  1. 1Enter the deal terms and upload the entities' balance sheets.
  2. 2See the result of each check, including the 4 : 1 debt-to-equity limit.
  3. 3Share the one-page report with the CFO.

Who decides: The CFO approves or changes the deal.

Data Privacy

Data Masking Gate for AI

Masks names, NIK, NPWP and bank accounts in the browser before data is sent to Claude, then restores the real values in the answer on screen only.

The problem: Staff paste salaries, NIK and NPWP numbers into AI tools without masking them.

Who uses it
Anyone in finance using AI
When
Every time data goes into an AI tool
Input
Any table or text with names, NIK, NPWP or bank accounts
Output
A masked version for Claude, with real values restored on your screen only

How it works

  1. 1Paste or upload the data.
  2. 2Check what was masked before it is sent.
  3. 3Read Claude's answer with the real values restored on your screen.

Who decides: You check the masked data before anything is sent.
